Report a problem

Spotted something broken, leaking or unsafe? Let the maintenance team know. It takes a few minutes and you don't need an account.

Safety tip

What to include

  • What the problem is, in a sentence or two
  • Where it is: drop a pin on the map or describe the building
  • A photo, if it is safe to take one
  • Your name and a phone number, if you'd like a call back (optional)
Open the report form

Is it urgent or dangerous?

Don't use the form for emergencies. If anyone is at risk, call emergency services first, then the after-hours line.

How it works

What happens after you report

Reports are checked by staff before they become work orders, so nothing is assigned automatically.

  1. You report

    Fill in the short form with a description, location and photo.

  2. Staff review

    The maintenance team checks each report and may call you for details.

  3. Work is scheduled

    Approved reports become work orders and are prioritised by safety and need.

  4. Fixed and closed

    Once the work is done it is recorded, so the community has a full history.

Something wrong?

Report it online any time, or call the office during working hours. If anyone is in danger, call emergency services first.
