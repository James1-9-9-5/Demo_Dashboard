Report a problem

Spotted something broken, leaking or unsafe? Let the maintenance team know. It takes a few minutes and you don't need an account.

Safety tip

What to include

  • What the problem is, in a sentence or two
  • Where it is: drop a pin on the map or describe the building
  • A photo, if it's safe to take one
  • Your name and phone number if you'd like a call back (optional)
Open the report form

Urgent or dangerous?

Don't use the form for emergencies. If anyone is at risk, call 911 first, then the after-hours line.

How it works

What happens after you report

Staff check every report before it becomes a work order, so nothing is assigned automatically.

  1. You report

    Fill in the short form with a description, location and photo.

  2. Staff review

    The maintenance team checks each report and may call you for details.

  3. Work is scheduled

    Approved reports become work orders, prioritised by safety and need.

  4. Fixed and closed

    Finished work is recorded, so the community keeps a full history.
